# Product

PermitTern: Organize every permit and renewal.

AI document management software that extracts permit conditions, renewal dates and required supporting documents for commercial organizations.

- Founder and CEO: Adetola Salako
- Co-founder and CTO: Chukwuma Nnamdi
- Industry: RegTech, Construction, Enterprise Software
- Customers: Construction companies, facilities operators and compliance consultancies
- Core features: Permit extraction, renewal tracking, missing document alerts, document comparison, review dashboards
- MVP: Permit uploads and renewal management
- Planned AWS: Textract, Bedrock, S3, DynamoDB, Lambda
- Business model: Organization and document-based subscriptions
- Main action: Book a demo
